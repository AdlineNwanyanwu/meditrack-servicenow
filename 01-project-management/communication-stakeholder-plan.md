
# MediTrack — Communication & Stakeholder Management Plan

## 1. Project Information

**Project:** MediTrack — Medical Equipment Management  
**Platform:** ServiceNow  
**Program:** ServiceNow RiseUp  
**Team:** Langston Team — Group 2  
**Team Size:** 5  
**Project Manager:** Adline Nwanyanwu  
**Status:** In Development

## 2. Purpose

This plan outlines the proposed communication and stakeholder engagement approach for the MediTrack project.

It is intended to support clear communication, team collaboration, progress visibility, and timely resolution of project dependencies.

## 3. Stakeholder Identification

| Stakeholder | Interest | Engagement Approach |
|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| Project Manager | Overall project delivery | Coordinate activities and monitor progress |
| Development Team | Assigned technical deliverables | Share updates and resolve dependencies |
| RiseUp Instructors | Project requirements and evaluation | Communicate progress and seek clarification |
| Clinical Staff (Simulated) | Equipment reporting and usability | Consider user experience requirements |
| Biomedical Engineers (Simulated) | Equipment repair management | Consider repair workflow requirements |
| Department Managers (Simulated) | Equipment visibility and oversight | Consider reporting and approval needs |
| System Administrators (Simulated) | Platform configuration and security | Consider access and maintenance requirements |

*Healthcare stakeholders represent intended application users, not confirmed external project participants.*

## 4. Communication Objectives

- Maintain clarity around project scope and responsibilities.
- Provide visibility into milestones and deliverables.
- Identify blockers and dependencies early.
- Support collaboration between technical workstreams.
- Communicate project decisions and changes.
- Prepare the team for integration testing and final presentation.

## 5. Proposed Communication Matrix

| Communication | Audience | Frequency | Purpose |
|---------------|----------|-----------|---------|
| Team Progress Check-In | Project Team | As agreed | Review progress and blockers |
| Milestone Review | Project Team | At phase completion | Validate deliverables |
| Technical Coordination | Relevant Team Members | As needed | Resolve dependencies |
| Risk and Issue Review | Project Team | Periodically | Track risks and issues |
| Instructor Updates | RiseUp Instructors | As required | Clarify requirements |
| Final Demo Preparation | Project Team | Before presentation | Coordinate demonstration |

*Frequencies are proposed and should be updated to reflect actual team practices.*

## 6. Communication Channels

The team may use the following channels, subject to agreement:

- Team meetings or virtual calls
- Group messaging
- Shared project documentation
- GitHub repository
- ServiceNow development instance

## 7. Roles and Responsibilities

### Project Manager

- Coordinate communication across team members.
- Maintain visibility into project milestones.
- Track significant decisions and blockers.
- Support alignment between workstreams.
- Coordinate testing and demonstration preparation.

### Team Members

- Communicate progress on assigned deliverables.
- Raise technical blockers and dependencies.
- Document significant configuration decisions.
- Participate in integration and testing activities.

## 8. Stakeholder Engagement Strategy

### Internal Project Team

Focus on:
- Shared understanding of requirements
- Clear ownership of deliverables
- Timely communication of dependencies
- Collaboration during testing

### Simulated Application Users

Consider the needs of:
- Clinical staff reporting faulty equipment
- Biomedical engineers managing repairs
- Facilities teams supporting maintenance
- Department managers monitoring equipment status

These perspectives will inform application requirements and usability decisions.

## 9. Decision Management

Significant project decisions should be documented with:

- Decision description
- Date
- Participants
- Rationale
- Impact on scope or deliverables

Decisions affecting multiple workstreams should be communicated to the relevant team members.

## 10. Escalation Process

1. Identify a blocker or concern.
2. Communicate it to the responsible team member.
3. Assess its impact on deliverables.
4. Coordinate a proposed resolution.
5. Escalate to the instructor when appropriate.
6. Document the outcome.

## 11. Communication Effectiveness

Communication effectiveness can be evaluated through:

- Clarity of assigned responsibilities
- Timely identification of blockers
- Completion of milestone reviews
- Reduced misunderstandings between workstreams
- Readiness for integration testing

## 12. Continuous Improvement

The communication approach will be reviewed as the project progresses.

Adjustments may be made based on team feedback, development challenges, and project deadlines.
