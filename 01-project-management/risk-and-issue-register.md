## 1. Purpose

This register provides a structured approach for identifying, evaluating, monitoring, and responding to risks and issues throughout the MediTrack project.

A risk is a potential future event that could affect project objectives.

An issue is a problem that has already occurred and requires action.

## 2. Risk Assessment Framework

Risks are evaluated using:

- **Probability:** Low, Medium, or High
- **Impact:** Low, Medium, or High
- **Priority:** Based on the combination of probability and impact

## 5. Issue Management Process

1. Identify and document the issue.
2. Assess its impact and urgency.
3. Assign an issue owner.
4. Determine corrective actions.
5. Monitor resolution progress.
6. Verify the solution.
7. Close the issue and document lessons learned.

## 6. Escalation Approach

Issues that threaten major project deliverables, dependencies, or deadlines should be communicated to the Project Manager.

The Project Manager coordinates with the relevant team member to determine corrective actions and communicate any necessary changes.

## 7. Monitoring and Review

The risk and issue register will be reviewed as project development progresses.

Updates may include:
- New risks
- Changes in probability or impact
- Newly identified issues
- Assigned owners
- Mitigation progress
- Resolution outcomes
